# Separate confirmed voice state from operation targets

Every public `VoiceConnectionState` snapshot will be a recursively frozen discriminated-union value. Its `confirmed` field is either null or an immutable projection of the latest authoritative current-bot voice state containing `channelId`, `mute`, `deaf`, `selfMute`, `selfDeaf`, `suppress`, and `requestToSpeakTimestamp`. Its `target` field exists only while `connecting` or `moving` and contains the requested `channelId`, `selfMute`, and `selfDeaf` values. This prevents requested intent from masquerading as Discord-confirmed membership.

`connecting` exposes `confirmed | null` plus `target`; `ready` and `recovering` expose a non-null `confirmed`; `moving` exposes both; and `destroyed` retains the final `confirmed | null` plus its destruction reason and a `VoiceError` only when the reason has an operational failure. ADR 0039 amends `disconnecting` to expose `confirmed | null` because an explicit disconnect may supersede initial connection before Discord confirms membership. A confirmed voice-state flag update produces a new snapshot and Seyfert state-change event even when `status` stays unchanged. Session IDs, voice tokens, endpoints, encryption material, SSRCs, and internal handshake phases remain private.
